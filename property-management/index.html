What's Included

Full-service
coverage.

Property management shouldn't be a second job. Lee coordinates every moving part — from finding the right tenant to handling maintenance calls — under one roof.

Tenant Placement

Listing, marketing, applicant screening, credit and background checks, and lease execution. Only qualified tenants move in.

Lease Administration

Lease drafting, renewals, amendments, and compliance with New York landlord-tenant law. Every document properly executed.

Rent Collection

Monthly collection, late notices, and escalation when needed. Consistent cash flow with full accounting transparency.

Maintenance & Repairs

Coordination of licensed contractors for routine upkeep and emergency repairs. Vetted vendors, fair pricing, no markups.

Code Compliance

Monitoring of NYC Housing Maintenance Code requirements, registration renewals, and inspection coordination.

Monthly Reporting

Clear monthly statements covering income, expenses, and outstanding items — everything you need to track your investment.
