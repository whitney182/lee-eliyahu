What It Is

Not an appraisal.
Better.

A Broker Opinion of Value is a broker's professional estimate of what a property would sell for in the current market. It's not a formal appraisal — it's faster, free, and often more useful for owners who are weighing their options.

Lee pulls recent comparable sales, reviews active competition, and applies local market knowledge to arrive at a realistic price range — not a number designed to win a listing, but one grounded in what buyers are actually paying.

Who requests a BOV Owners considering a sale
  Estate & probate situations
  Investors evaluating acquisitions
  Refinance & equity decisions
Delivery format Written summary + call
Obligation to list None
What's Covered

Six angles,
one picture.

Every BOV Lee delivers covers the full picture — not just a number, but the reasoning behind it so you can make a confident decision about what's next.

Comparable Sales

Recent closed transactions in your submarket — adjusted for size, condition, location, and timing — to establish a baseline value.

Active Competition

Where your property sits relative to what buyers can see right now — and how that positioning affects pricing strategy and days on market.

Absorption Rate

How fast similar properties are going into contract — so you understand timing, seasonality, and realistic expectations before deciding to sell.

Pricing Strategy

A recommended list price range — priced to attract buyers and close, not inflated to win the listing. Includes rationale for high, low, and target price.

Condition Adjustments

How the property's current condition, age, recent improvements, and any deferred maintenance factor into the final value range.

Net Proceeds Estimate

A rough seller-side breakdown — estimated closing costs, broker commissions, and what you'd realistically walk away with at different price points.
