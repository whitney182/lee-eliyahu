Loan Programs

Six programs
for investors.

From a quick residential flip to a ground-up development, there's a private lending structure built for the deal — not a generic product forced to fit it.

Fix & Flip

Short-term bridge loans designed for residential investors buying, renovating, and reselling. Fast close, interest-only payments during the hold, and terms structured around your project timeline.

Land Acquisition

Financing for raw land, infill lots, and development sites where conventional lenders typically won't go. Asset-based underwriting means the deal can move even on unimproved parcels.

Ground-Up Construction

New build financing for developers and builders. Draw schedules aligned with construction milestones, with terms flexible enough to accommodate extended project timelines.

Bridge Loans

Short-term capital to bridge a gap — between purchase and refinance, between sell and buy, or while a project stabilizes ahead of permanent financing. Structured for speed and flexibility.

Cash-Out Refinance

Pull equity from an existing investment property quickly — without waiting months for a bank appraisal cycle. Ideal for investors recycling capital into the next acquisition.

Multi-Family & Investment

Private lending for 2–4 unit and 5+ unit investment properties — acquisitions, value-add repositioning, and portfolio expansion where the deal economics justify speed over rate.
